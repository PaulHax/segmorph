One segmentation, three representations at once

A chest CT with two overlapping segments. Each segment is stored as a binary labelmap, converted to a surface for the 3D view with labelmapToSurface, and cut to contours on the slice with surfaceToContour. Every conversion runs in a web worker as you change the controls.

Detail i

The voxel stride every conversion runs at, resampled with resampleNearest. This feeds all the other controls, so it changes both views.

Full resolution on this volume is a few million triangles and takes seconds; half is a good interactive default.

Segments i

Body and Bone are two segmentations of this CT, seeded by thresholding intensity. Unchecking one hides it in both views.

They deliberately overlap: every bone voxel is also a body voxel. One fused labelmap cannot express that, since a voxel holds a single value, so each segment carries its own mask and label value.

Surface processing i

smooth runs meshSmooth, a windowed-sinc filter that relaxes the voxel staircase marching cubes leaves. It moves vertices without changing how many there are.

decimate runs meshDecimate, quadric edge-collapse, dropping the triangles that flattened regions no longer need.
